This device complies with Part 15 of the FCC Rules. Operation is subject to the following two conditions: (1) this device may not cause harmful interference, and (2) this device must accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.

Battery & charging

The sensor contains a sealed rechargeable lithium-polymer cell. Charge only with the supplied cable and a standard USB power source. Do not puncture, crush, incinerate, or disassemble the sensor. Store between −10°C and 45°C. Do not dispose of in household waste — recycle through a local battery collection point.

Warranty

One year from delivery against defects in materials and workmanship. Normal wear, impact damage, and water ingress outside the rated seal are not covered. Contact support to start a claim.

Returns

Unused hardware can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the purchase price. Pre-order reservations can be cancelled any time before your unit ships.

Privacy

Your faceoff data belongs to you. We collect what the app needs to work and what you choose to send us. We do not sell personal data. You can request deletion of your account and its data at any time by emailing support.
